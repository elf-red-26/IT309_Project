Admission Requirements

For incoming freshmen, the following are the requirements for admission:

  • Form 137 (Report Card)
  • PSA Birth Certificate (Photocopy)
  • Good Moral Certificate (Original copy)
  • 2pcs 2x2 Picture
  • Long Brown Envelope

For transferees, the following are the requirements for admission:

  • Transfer Credentials (Original copy)
  • Transcript of Records (Original copy)
  • PSA Birth Certificate (Photocopy)
  • Good Moral Certificate (Original copy)
  • 2x2 Picture
  • Long Brown Envelope
